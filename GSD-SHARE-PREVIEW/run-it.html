The right size

Most small teams don't need a full-time hire.

A 12-person org rarely has 40 hours a week of IT work. It has about 8 good hours and a lot of quiet gaps. GSD covers those hours, sized to the work you have. The team runs the day to day and Shiraz, as fractional CIO/CTO, takes the calls that matter.
