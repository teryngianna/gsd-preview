Shiraz Karmaz

Co-founder, Fractional CIO/CTO

I've spent twenty years running engineering and security, mostly in healthcare, where the rules are strict and the consequences are real. I started GSD because the organizations I most wanted to help couldn't hire a technology department and shouldn't have to.

On fractional work you get me directly. For day-to-day IT and builds, I scope, plan, and design the work, and the team carries it out. I step back as it stops needing me.

The best thing I do happens in the hard rooms. When a decision has stalled and everyone is talking past each other, I'm the one who says let's get one shoe on, then the next.
