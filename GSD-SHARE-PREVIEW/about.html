Same senior judgment. Sized to where you are.

GSD is a fractional technology practice and an engineering team, built for mission-driven organizations. The people doing work that matters shouldn't have to choose between senior help and a budget they can live with.

Who's behind it

Shiraz Karmaz

Co-founder, Fractional CIO/CTO

I've spent twenty years running engineering and security, mostly in healthcare, where the rules are strict and the consequences are real. I started GSD because the organizations I most wanted to help couldn't hire a technology department and shouldn't have to. Today I'm also CTO at ForthBridge.

On fractional work you get me directly. For day-to-day IT and builds, I scope, plan, and design the work, and the team carries it out. I step back as it stops needing me.

Our approach

Four things that hold on every engagement.

Senior judgment, plus the execution

Shiraz is on every engagement. On fractional work you get him directly. On day-to-day IT and builds he scopes, plans, and designs the work, and the team carries it out. No junior handoff once the ink is dry.

We start with the outcome

Before anyone builds anything, we get clear on what you're solving for. The right work gets a lot cheaper once we agree on where you're going, and you feel progress in weeks.

Built to need us less

We teach your team to carry the routine and write the exit into the engagement from day one. We'd rather earn the next call than keep you dependent.

Plain language, always

Honest tradeoffs, no acronym soup. You'll always know what matters, what can wait, and why.

Why mission-driven work

We choose mission-driven work on purpose.

Nonprofits and B Corps, founders building something with weight behind it, teams scaling under real stakes. What they share is a reason for the work beyond the work itself, and technology that has to hold up while they get on with it.

Mission-driven orgs get aligned pricing. The same senior people do the work, shaped to a nonprofit budget, because we want your energy on the mission while your technology holds up underneath you.
